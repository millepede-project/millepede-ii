## Collection of users

This list is probably incomplete - you are very welcome to add yourself or get added!

For a recent and detailed description of a complex use case see the [Belle II thesis of T. Bilka.](https://dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/178726).

### CMS

CMS has been the main user driving the developments during LHC run 1.
- Strategies and performance of the CMS silicon tracker alignment during LHC Run 2,
  CMS Collaboration, NIM A, 1037 (2022), 166795, [doi: 10.1016/j.nima.2022.166795](http://doi.org/10.1016/j.nima.2022.166795)
- Alignment of the CMS tracker with LHC and cosmic ray data, CMS collaboration, 
  [2014 JINST 9 P06009](http://iopscience.iop.org/1748-0221/9/06/P06009)
- Alignment of the CMS silicon tracker during commissioning with cosmic rays, 
  [2010 JINST 5 T03009](http://iopscience.iop.org/1748-0221/5/03/T03009)
- Alignment of the CMS silicon strip tracker during stand-alone commissioning, W. Adam et 
  al., [2009 JINST 4 T07001](http://iopscience.iop.org/1748-0221/4/07/T07001)
- CMS silicon tracker alignment strategy with the Millepede II algorithm, G. Flucke,
  P. Schleper, G. Steinbrück, M. Stoye et al., 
  [2008 JINST 3 P09002](http://iopscience.iop.org/1748-0221/3/09/P09002)

### Belle II
In the first years of operation Belle II has become the second main user.
- Status of the calibration and alignment framework at the Belle II experiment, 
  D. Dossett et al 2017 J. Phys.: Conf. Ser. 898 032032, 
  [doi:10.1088/1742-6596/898/3/032032](http://dx.doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/898/3/032032)
- Time-dependent CP violation in B<sup>0</sup> → η<sub>c</sub> K<sup>0</sup><sub>S</sub> decays at the Belle experiment, Alignment of the Belle II detector, [Doctoral Thesis T. Bilka, Charles University Prague (2022)](https://dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/178726)

### CrystFEL
- The [CrystFEL software](https://www.desy.de/~twhite/crystfel) for
serial X-ray crystallography uses Millepede-II for detector alignment
since version 0.11.0. [Read more here](https://gitlab.desy.de/thomas.white/crystfel/-/blob/master/doc/man/align_detector.1.md).
